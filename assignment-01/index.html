My Top 5 Favorite Movies

  1. The Birdcage

    A South Beach nightclub owner and his long-time partner and drag headliner must pretend to be a traditional straight family.

  2. Fight Club

    An unnamed office worker suffering from insomnia forms an underground fighting group with a charismatic soap salesman as an escape from corporate consumerism.

  3. Resident Evil

    The corrupt Umbrella Corporation creates dangerous bio-organic weapons, mutations, and viruses that turn people into monsters and zombies.

  4. Asteroid City

    A play within a play about a 1955 junior stargazing convention in a fictional desert town.

  5. Nope

    Two ranch-owning siblings try to capture video evidence of a mysterious UFO hiding in the clouds above their California ranch.
