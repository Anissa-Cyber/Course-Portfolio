My Top 5 Favorite Movies

  1. The Birdcage

    A South Beach nightclub owner and his long-time partner and drag headliner must pretend to be a traditional straight family.

    Fight Club

    An unnamed office worker suffers from insomnia forms an underground fighting group with a charismatic soap salesman as an escape from corporate consumerism.

    Resident Evil

    The dark work of the corrupt Umbrella Corporation creates dangerous bio-organic weapons, mutations, and viruses that turn people into monsters and zombies.

    Asteroid City

    A play within a pay about a 1955 junior stargazing convention in a fictional desert town.

    Nope

    Two ranch owning siblings try to capture video evidence of a mysterious UFO hiding in the clouds above their California ranch.
